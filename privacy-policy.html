Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Privacy A brief statement explaining that we value your trust and aim to be transparent about how we handle your data.

Introduction

Welcome to Oak. At Oak, accessible from our website, the privacy of our visitors is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document contains detailed information about the types of information that are collected and recorded by Oak and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us. By accessing or using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies only to online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect on Oak. This policy is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website. This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content and attachments
  • Any other information you choose to provide

You may choose to visit our website anonymously. However, certain services may require you to provide personal information to function properly.

Automatically Collected Information

Like many websites, Oak automatically collects certain non-personal information when visitors access the site. This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited on our website
  • Date and time of visit
  • Referring/exit pages
  • Device type and operating system

This data is used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users’ movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

How We Use Your Information

Oak uses the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Enhance user experience and personalize content
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Respond to customer service requests and inquiries
  • Send administrative information and updates
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • Ensure compliance with legal obligations

Cookies and Web Beacons

Oak uses cookies to store information about visitors’ preferences and to optimize the experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors’ browser type or other information.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. More detailed information about cookie management can be found on your browser’s help page.

Google AdSense and Advertising Partners

Oak uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads to users based on their visit to our website and other websites on the internet.

These cookies allow Google and its partners to display advertisements that may be relevant to your interests. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Oak’s Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers or websites for more detailed information about their practices and instructions on how to opt out of certain options.

CCPA Privacy Rights (California Consumers)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of data collected about them
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Request that personal data not be sold

Log Files

Oak follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.
