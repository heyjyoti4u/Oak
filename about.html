About Oak

We are a community of passionate travelers who believe journeys shape stories, create memories, and connect souls across the world.

Nice to meet you

We are Akanksha, Srumitha, and Jyoti — three explorers brought together by a shared love for discovering the unknown. Oak was born from our desire to inspire people to travel deeper, slower, and more meaningfully.

We believe travel is not about ticking places off a list, but about creating experiences that stay in your heart forever.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage travelers to explore the world with curiosity, respect, and passion. We aim to build a platform where journeys become stories and destinations become memories.
