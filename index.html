Travel Diaries

Neelima Vallangi

photographer and solo traveller

“
“ One of her most memorable was her trek to Tso Moriri in Ladakh via the challenging Parang La pass. This 10-day trek took her from Spiti to Ladakh, crossing high-altitude terrains and navigating a snow-covered pass at 5,600 meters. After days of grueling hiking, a stunning high-altitude lake that welcomed her with its serene turquoise waters and vibrant grasslands.”

Ajay Sood

Explorer and Astro-photographer

“
“ His photography has garnered significant acclaim, including recognition as the 2016 Outlook Social Media (OSM) Photographer of the Year and winning multiple awards, such as the 2018 PhotoBlogger of the Year from Gujarat Tourism. Additionally, his short stories have earned him accolades like the 2019 Earthys Award for Best Travel Short Story. Sood has built a reputation through contributions to esteemed travel. ”

Mohit Manocha

Mountain Trekker and Solo travel vlogger

“
“ During one of his trips, he encountered unexpected challenges when the motorhome he initially purchased broke down just before the journey began. Undeterred, he rented another motorhome and carried on with his travels, showcasing his adaptability and dedication to his content creation. Later, he embarked on another ambitious project: acquiring a dream motorhome from the USA with plans to travel through Europe. ”

Varun Vagish

Mountain Trekker and travel Vlogger

“
“ One particularly remarkable adventure was his backpacking journey through remote areas of Bhutan. What made this experience unique was his commitment to exploring offbeat locations, interacting with locals, and traveling on a minimal budget to show that meaningful travel doesn't require luxury. His focus on immersing in the local culture and documenting authentic, lesser-known experiences has set him apart as a travel vlogger. ”

Deepanshu Sangwan

Hitchhikes and Solo traveler

“
“ One of his most cherished travel experiences took place in Iran, where he hitchhiked across the country. This adventure stands out not only for the thrill but also for the deep cultural exchanges he encountered. Sangwan's openness to engaging with locals and embracing unexpected moments made the journey memorable. Iran’s hospitality, where strangers offered him food and a place to stay, left a lasting impact on him, highlighting how travel can break down stereotypes and foster meaningful connection”

Kritika Goel

Travel and lifestyle blogger

“
“One of Kritika’s most celebrated travel experiences was her trek to Kedarkantha, a stunning peak in Uttarakhand, India. Here’s a glimpse into her journey. The trek took her through beautiful pine forests, vast meadows, and snow-laden paths. Kritika described the breathtaking views of the snow-covered landscape and the thrill of walking through knee-deep snow, which added an adventurous touch to her journey.”

Tanya Khanijow

Solo Travel Vlogger

“
“ During this trip, she explored picturesque locations like Paro, Thimphu, and Punakha. A highlight of her adventure was hiking a physically demanding trek that offers breathtaking views of the Paro valley. This journey challenged her physically and mentally, but reaching the monastery gave her a deep sense of accomplishment and connection to the serene environment. Tanya emphasizes that Bhutan’s blend of natural beauty and rich culture left a lasting impression on her.”
